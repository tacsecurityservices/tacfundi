01

Overview & Application

TACFundi ("we", "us", or "our") provides technical cybersecurity audits, POPIA compliance frameworks, and digital risk management services for high-stakes business environments, notably legal and medical firms. This policy details how our website, portals, and diagnostic tools treat your data assets.

By engaging with Our website, you acknowledge the configurations, processing schedules, and verification checks detailed within this document.

02

Practice and Client Safeguards

Firms handling patient records or sensitive legal cases require higher assurance layers. We implement strict operational parameters when assessing and testing client platforms:

Legal Professional Privilege

Any corporate metadata generated during digital threat mapping or system evaluations remains fully covered under privilege protocols and is never logged on public infrastructure.

Medical Record Sovereignty

Diagnostic evaluations and technical configurations strictly avoid the aggregation of direct, identifiable healthcare records or patient histories.

03

Information We Process

To facilitate comprehensive technical reviews and portal experiences, we process:

  • • Professional Information: Firm names, CIPC validation states, practice ID numbers, email communication nodes, and phone lines.
  • • Vulnerability Logs: Assessment reports, network configuration ratings, open diagnostic tests, and encryption scores collected via client consent.
  • • Browsing Data: Connection metadata, device operating metrics, performance telemetry, and diagnostic cookies.
04

POPIA Compliance Mandates

In strict compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa, we uphold the 8 legal Processing Conditions:

Accountability: We assume active duty of care for data held in our trust.
Specification of Purpose: Only processing data for cybersecurity diagnosis.
Openness: Complete transparency of automated scripts and diagnostic scanners.
05

Secure Encryption Protocols

Unencrypted file transfers are primary leak pathways in SA firms. TACFundi mitigates this exposure pattern directly:

"All diagnostic files, assessment reports, and connection sessions are run inside secured containerized architecture and guarded by AES-256 standard encryption algorithms during processing and static storage."
