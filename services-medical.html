The Healthcare Data Challenge

Your patients trust you with their most sensitive information.

Every patient file you hold contains medical histories, diagnosis records, ID numbers, financial details, and sometimes deeply personal information about their lives. Under POPIA, you are legally required to protect all of it.

Most medical practices already take patient confidentiality seriously. But the POPIA obligation goes further: it requires formal data processing documentation, staff training records, and a breach response plan.

TACFundi handles the entire compliance implementation for you. No disruption to your practice. No technical jargon. No need to become a cybersecurity expert.
