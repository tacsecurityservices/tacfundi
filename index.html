The Risk Is Real

The law is clear. The exposure is real. The window is closing.

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act, your practice is legally required to protect every piece of client or patient personal information you hold. This isn't optional guidance — it's a legal obligation with serious consequences for non-compliance.

Most South African law firms and medical practices handle this data every day without proper protection in place. Most have been meaning to address it for months — or years.

TACFundi exists for exactly this moment. We are the specialists who make compliance straightforward, affordable, and done — so you can focus on your clients.
