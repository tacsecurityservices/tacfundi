The Legal Obligation

Your clients' data is your responsibility. POPIA makes it law.

Every property transfer, divorce settlement, commercial contract, and estate matter your firm handles contains sensitive personal information. Under POPIA, you are legally required to protect it — and to have demonstrable systems in place to do so.

The Information Regulator is active. Fines are being issued. Client data breaches are now among the leading causes of professional negligence claims in the South African legal sector.

TACFundi specialises in helping law firms meet their POPIA obligations without disrupting the practice or requiring a single technical conversation. We handle the complexity so you don't have to.
